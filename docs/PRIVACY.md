# Privacy policy for Pinyin Chart

Last updated: [DATE]

Pinyin Chart ("the app") is made by [DEVELOPER NAME OR COMPANY] ("we"). This policy explains what happens to information when you use the app on Android or on the web at [WEB ADDRESS, IF ANY].

## The short version

- The app has no account, no sign-in, no advertising, no analytics and no tracking.
- Everything you add stays on your device. We never receive it.
- The Android app does not connect to the internet at all.

## What the app stores, and where

On your device only, in the app's own storage:

- your statuses for each syllable and tone (how well you know it) and your progress in Learn;
- your notes;
- your voice recordings;
- characters, meanings and example words you edit;
- your settings (appearance, text size and similar).

None of this is sent to us or to anyone else. It is deleted if you use **Reset app** (in My data), clear the app's storage, or uninstall the app. On Android it is not included in Google's automatic backup, so it does not move to a new phone unless you export a backup.

## Microphone

The app asks for the microphone only when you tap **Record**, so you can record yourself and compare your pronunciation with the native recording. Recordings are saved on your device and analysed there (for the pitch curve and waveform). They are never uploaded. You can refuse or withdraw access at any time in your device settings; everything else in the app keeps working.

## Files you export

**Export backup** and **Export cards for Anki** create a file on your device and, on Android, open the system share sheet. Where the file goes next (for example Google Drive, e-mail or a file manager) is your choice, and that service's own privacy policy applies to it. We do not receive these files.

**Restore from backup** reads a file you choose; it is processed on your device.

## Links to other websites

"Hear it on Forvo" and the links on the Credits page open in your browser. Those websites are not run by us and have their own privacy policies. The app does not send them anything except what your browser sends when it opens a page.
